Quick Start Guide

Follow these steps to use the configuration provided above in your UseGalaxy PULSAR environment:

1
Copy Credentials
Use the copy buttons on the dashboard to copy the Galaxy Username and Password.
2
Open UseGalaxy
Log in to your designated UseGalaxy PULSAR instance.
3
Configure Connection
Navigate to the connection settings and paste the credentials into the respective fields.
4
Verify Quotas
Ensure the Max CPUs, RAM, and GPUs match your assigned resource limits.
Tip: If you encounter connection issues, please verify that your network allows traffic to the PULSAR gateway and that the credentials have not expired.
